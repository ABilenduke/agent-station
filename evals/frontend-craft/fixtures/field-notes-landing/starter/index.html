A working studio. A welcoming table.

We are a small independent print studio at 18 Foundry Lane, Hamilton. Workshops take place around a shared table with step-free access from the street and an accessible washroom.

Wear something you would not mind getting a little ink on. We provide aprons, tools, paper, and tea. If you have access needs, add a note when reserving and we will get in touch.
