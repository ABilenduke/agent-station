## Changes the runs led to

- `worklog post` refuses to replace a `time` comment that reports steps the local ledger lacks.
- `worklog issue section` and `worklog issue comment` print the current content without `--file`.
- The contract says to pass content without markers, and to open a sub-issue when an existing issue
  covers more than the piece of work.
- Agreeing to open or adopt an issue covers its record from then on, so resuming does not re-ask.
- A research correction revises the plan (Revisions line, steps waiting on the reopened decision)
  instead of leaving it alone.
