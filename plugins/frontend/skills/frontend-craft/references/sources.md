## What was adapted

This skill is a rewritten synthesis, not an upstream distribution or an endorsement by its authors. The following describes its modifications:

- **Art direction:** Combine Anthropic's subject-led composition and restraint with Taste's attention to audience, existing assets, density, and motion. Replace framework defaults, aesthetic bans, and imagery quotas with decisions tied to the brief.
- **Product interfaces:** Adapt Impeccable's distinction between operational and expressive work, emphasis on useful density, and consistent component language. Keep established identity and APIs unless the user's scope permits replacement; state guidance applies only to states the component can reach.
- **HTML/CSS and accessibility:** Select applicable Vercel and Impeccable checks, then qualify them using platform behavior and W3C criteria. Native controls do not require redundant keyboard handlers; target-size thresholds distinguish WCAG AA requirements from larger ergonomic recommendations; virtualization depends on actual need.
- **Rendered review:** Retain Impeccable's separation of visual judgment and mechanical evidence. Replace mandatory runtimes, detectors, hook installation, concept-selection ceremonies, and fixed report formats with direct inspection and targeted correction through available tools.
- **SEO:** Add purpose-specific public-page guidance informed by Google Search Central. Preserve private and staging indexing intent.

No upstream application code, fonts, images, or platform-specific reference material is bundled. License and notice files are retained unmodified; see [adaptation notices](../third-party/NOTICE.md). The candidate's effectiveness must be established by comparative evaluation, not by the reputation of these sources.

## Standards and platform references

These are authoritative references rather than vendored text. Numerical accessibility guidance follows the dated recommendation and its explanatory documents, not third-party skill checklists.

- [WCAG 2.2, W3C Recommendation of 12 December 2024](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/): default accessibility target and conformance scope.
- WAI explanations for [contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html), [target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html), and [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html): exceptions and practical interpretation.
- [WAI modal dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/): focus and dismissal behavior; informative implementation guidance, not a standalone conformance test.
- [MDN minimum sizing](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/min-width): automatic minimum size behavior for flex and grid items.
- Google Search Central: [JavaScript SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics), [title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link), and [robots.txt](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro).
